### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary
Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **asset lifecycle management platforms**, **open-source EAM/ITAM frameworks**, and **fixed asset management tools**. Whether you are looking for enterprise-grade commercial solutions (such as *IBM Maximo*, *ServiceNow ITAM*, and *SAP EAM*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *CalemEAM*, *openMAINT*, and *Snipe-IT*), this list covers category leaders, asset registry, and privacy-respecting asset management.

**Key Market Context:**
- **IBM Maximo Application Suite** is a **modular enterprise EAM/APM platform** with distinct modules (Maximo Manage, Predict, Monitor, Visual Inspection), AI via IBM WatsonX, and SaaS Essentials starting at **~$40,000–$47,000/year** per component [citation:12].
- **CalemEAM** is the **leading open-source full EAM platform** with **asset tracking, work orders, preventive maintenance, and purchasing integration** — GPLv2 licensed for medium-to-large manufacturers [citation:9].
- **openMAINT** builds on **CMDBuild** for **facility and plant asset management** with **asset registry, work orders, energy tracking, and IoT sensor integration** — AGPLv3 licensed [citation:9].
- **Snipe-IT** is the **most popular open-source IT asset tracking tool**, with **asset registration, assignment, auditing, and reporting** — ideal for small to medium IT teams [citation:4][citation:13].
- **Ralph** is the **open-source DCIM/CMDB leader** with **2,520+ GitHub stars**, **interactive data center floor plans**, **rack visualization**, **license compliance**, and **workflow transitions** — Apache 2.0 licensed [citation:8][citation:18].
- **GLPI** combines **ITSM and asset management** with **helpdesk, inventory, and contract management** — the best fit for mid-sized IT teams needing tickets alongside assets [citation:4][citation:19].

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Ralph](https://github.com/allegro/ralph)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/allegro/ralph?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/allegro/ralph/stargazers)  
  **Open-source CMDB, DCIM, and asset management platform**, Apache 2.0 licensed. **2,520+ GitHub stars, 594 forks** — **used by Allegro in production** [citation:8]. **Interactive data center floor plans** with drag-and-drop rack placement, front and rear rack views showing U-level asset positioning, power consumption tracking per PDU, and ethernet and fiber connection mapping [citation:8]. **Back office module** manages laptops, desktops, printers, mobile phones, and accessories with user assignment tracking, warehouse inventory, stock-taking audits, and automated loan workflows [citation:8]. **IP address management** with DHCP and DNS integration. **Software license tracking** with per-user and per-core allocations, compliance reporting, and expiration alerts [citation:8]. **Transitions system** automates asset lifecycle processes through configurable workflows triggering HTTP webhooks, Python scripts, email notifications, and Puppet/Ansible integration [citation:8]. **Built on Python 3 with Django and Django REST Framework** — deploy via Docker with MySQL and Redis [citation:8]. **The cleanest open-source option for data center and rack-level asset management** [citation:18]. 🗄️

- **[CalemEAM](https://github.com/calemead/calemeam)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/calemead/calemeam?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/calemead/calemeam/stargazers)  
  **Full enterprise-scale asset management with modular design**, GPLv2 licensed. **The leading open-source full EAM platform** — **asset tracking, work orders, preventive maintenance, and purchasing integration** [citation:9]. **Web-based interface** supports multi-site operations, role-based access, and analytics dashboards [citation:9]. **Used by medium to large manufacturers, utilities, and facilities groups** [citation:9]. 🏭

- **[openMAINT](https://github.com/openMAINT/openMAINT)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openMAINT/openMAINT?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openMAINT/openMAINT/stargazers)  
  **Facility and plant asset management with strong maintenance control**, AGPLv3 licensed. **Builds on the CMDBuild framework** — **asset registry, work orders, energy tracking, and document management** [citation:9]. **Integrates with IoT sensors via connectors** [citation:9]. **Used by industrial sites and public infrastructure organizations across Europe** [citation:9]. 🏢

- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/snipe/snipe-it?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it/stargazers)  
  **Lightweight IT asset management with IT and equipment focus**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **The most popular open-source IT asset tracking tool** — **asset registration, assignment, auditing, and reporting** [citation:4][citation:13]. **Tracks location, owner, warranty, and check-in/check-out history** [citation:9]. **Technical services and maintenance departments** [citation:9]. **Ideal for small to medium IT teams with light workflow needs** [citation:4]. 📋

- **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/glpi-project/glpi?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi/stargazers)  
  **ITSM + asset management + automatic discovery**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Combines helpdesk, inventory, and contract management** [citation:4][citation:19]. **GLPI Agent for automatic inventory** [citation:18]. **Best for mid-sized IT teams that need tickets alongside asset tracking** [citation:18]. 🎫

- **[ERPNext (Asset Module)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)  
  **All-in-one ERP with integrated asset management**, GPLv3 licensed. **Covers procurement, depreciation, maintenance, and insurance** [citation:9]. **Combines accounting with maintenance scheduling and cost tracking** [citation:9]. **Best for SMEs integrating asset and production management in one system** [citation:9]. 🏢

- **[i-doit](https://github.com/i-doit/i-doit)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/i-doit/i-doit?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/i-doit/i-doit/stargazers)  
  **ITIL-compliant CMDB and IT documentation**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Every server, application, contract, location, and person is a configuration item (CI)** . **Impact analysis — "if this database goes down, which services and customers are affected"** [citation:18]. **Extensible data model through XML** [citation:18]. **Best for regulated environments where change management depends on knowing the dependency graph** [citation:18]. 🔗

- **[OCS Inventory NG](https://github.com/OCSInventory-NG/OCSInventory-Server)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OCSInventory-NG/OCSInventory-Server?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/OCSInventory-NG/OCSInventory-Server/stargazers)  
  **Automatic hardware and software inventory**, GPL-2.0 licensed. **Agent-based discovery of hardware specs, installed software with versions, network configuration, and Windows registry data** [citation:18]. **Weakness: UI and reporting layer look dated** — **many teams pair OCS with GLPI for the workflow layer** [citation:18]. 🔍

- **[BaseEAM](https://github.com/BaseEAM/BaseEAM)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/BaseEAM/BaseEAM?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/BaseEAM/BaseEAM/stargazers)  
  **Asset lifecycle management with financial insight**, Apache 2.0 licensed. **Focuses on asset accounting, condition monitoring, and capital project tracking** [citation:9]. **Supports REST APIs and cloud deployment** [citation:9]. **Best for manufacturing and energy companies implementing digital asset strategies** [citation:9]. 💰

- **[SAMS](https://github.com/karthik558/SAMS)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/karthik558/SAMS?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/karthik558/SAMS/stargazers)  
  **Smart Asset Management System with QR-enabled tracking**, open-source. **Streamlines the full lifecycle of physical assets across properties and departments** — **register, track, audit, and label assets with QR-based workflows** [citation:5]. **React 18, TypeScript, Supabase** — **asset registry, properties, QR codes, tickets, reports, and role management** [citation:5]. **PWA-ready with offline asset list caching** [citation:5]. 📱

- **[Nexus\Assets](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-assets)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/azaharizaman/nexus-assets?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/azaharizaman/nexus-assets/stargazers)  
  **Framework-agnostic fixed asset management with progressive delivery**, open-source. **Three tiers**: **Basic (SB)** — simple tracking and straight-line depreciation; **Advanced (MB)** — double declining balance, maintenance/warranty tracking, multi-location inventory, TCO analysis; **Enterprise (LE)** — units-of-production depreciation, physical audits, multi-currency, automatic GL posting [citation:10]. **Asset lifecycle: Acquisition → Active → Disposal** [citation:10]. **GAAP-compliant daily prorating for mid-month acquisitions** [citation:10]. 🏛️

- **[Fixed Asset Management System (Spring Boot + Vue.js)](https://github.com/felix9611/springboot-fixedasset-vue-2-pgsql)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/felix9611/springboot-fixedasset-vue-2-pgsql?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/felix9611/springboot-fixedasset-vue-2-pgsql/stargazers)  
  **Cloud-based fixed asset management**, open-source. **Spring Boot 2.4.13 + Vue.js 2.6.10 + MySQL 8** . **Purchase and maintenance record management, asset write-off tracking, status monitoring, and reporting** [citation:15]. **JWT-based authentication** . **Chart.js for real-time data visualization** . **No installation required — cloud-based** [citation:15]. ☁️

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **IBM Maximo Essentials starts at ~$40K–$47K/year per component** with **12-month non-cancellable commitment**, and **average implementation takes ~7 months** according to reviewers [citation:12]. **MaintainX offers a free tier** with **paid plans at $20 and $65/user/month and no long-term contracts** [citation:12].
- **Open-source asset management is not free** — **CalemEAM and openMAINT have zero license cost** but **require technical expertise to deploy, customize, and maintain** [citation:14]. **Snipe-IT and OCS are the lightest to run** . **GLPI is heavier and needs database tuning at scale** [citation:18].
- **Choose based on your primary need**: **Snipe-IT for simple IT asset tracking**, **GLPI for ITSM + assets**, **Ralph for data center/rack-level DCIM**, **CalemEAM for full EAM**, **openMAINT for facility management**, **i-doit for CMDB and impact analysis**, **ERPNext for ERP-integrated asset management** [citation:18][citation:19].
- **Always validate asset tracking, depreciation calculations, and workflow automation with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment. 🔄
